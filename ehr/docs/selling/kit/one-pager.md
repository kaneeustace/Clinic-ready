## Who it's for

- Outpatient mental health and substance use clinics with roughly 1 to 20 clinicians
- Intensive outpatient (IOP) programs that track level of care and utilization review
- Clinics that bill AHCCCS (Arizona Medicaid) and commercial insurance
- Clinics with associate-level clinicians, BHTs or BHPPs whose notes need a supervisor's co-signature

## The problem

- **Audits and recoupments.** A missing start time, an expired treatment plan or a cloned note can turn a paid claim into a payback.
- **Unsigned notes pile up.** Nobody notices until billing is held or an auditor asks.
- **Intake is scattered.** Paper forms, insurance card photos in someone's phone, consents in a folder.
- **Level of care is tracked in spreadsheets.** Reviews and authorizations expire without warning.

## What ChartReady does

### Clinical documentation and treatment plans
- Intake, progress (DAP/SOAP), group, psychiatry, contact, discharge, safety plan, ASAM and psychotherapy notes.
- Structured mental status exam and risk assessment, checked before signing.
- Treatment plans with problems, goals, objectives and interventions.
- **Golden thread:** each session note records which plan objectives it addressed. Progress and group notes can't be written until a signed, active treatment plan is on file.
- Signing requires the clinician's password. Signed notes can't be changed; corrections go in addenda.

### AHCCCS-aware documentation and billing checks
- AHCCCS fields appear only for members with an AHCCCS ID: actual start and stop times, service type, place of service, telehealth modality (GT/FQ), participants, interpreter, late-entry flag and more.
- Warns when a note's narrative is identical or nearly identical to an earlier note (cloned documentation).
- Charge-time checks: units match minutes, telehealth modifier matches the note, plan and assessment are current, a minor's plan has a guardian signature.

### AI insurance-compliance note review
- One click on a note returns findings against payer documentation expectations, before you bill.
- **Note scanner:** upload a PDF note from another system (typed, scanned or handwritten), pick the payer (commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, AHCCCS) and get the same findings. The PDF is not stored.
- Advisory only. Requires a signed BAA with the AI provider before it is turned on for real patient data.

### Signature deadlines and supervisor co-signs
- A workboard on the dashboard: sessions needing a note, drafts to sign, co-signatures owed, each with a live countdown.
- Notes from clinicians who aren't independently licensed route to their supervisor for co-signature.
- **End-of-day emails** tell each clinician what's still open before they leave. No patient information in the email.

### Level of care, utilization review and census
- Current level of care (with ASAM level and program) in the chart banner, step-up/step-down history, payer authorizations with days and sessions used.
- Alerts for upcoming utilization reviews and expiring authorizations.
- A Census page showing who is at each level on any date.

### Check-in without the clipboard
- **Phone check-in:** clients confirm contact details, add new insurance with card photos, complete PHQ-9/GAD-7 when due, and sign consent.
- **Automatic check-in links** go out before each appointment by email or text.
- **Front-desk iPad kiosk** that resets itself between clients.
- Returning clients get a one-tap "Welcome back" check-in.

### Patient photos
- Profile photo in the chart banner for identification, plus photo ID and insurance card images.

### Installable phone app
- Installs to the home screen on iPhone and Android. Opens full screen and updates itself. No patient data is cached on the phone.

### Security
- Role-based access, two-factor sign-in, automatic sign-out after inactivity, a tamper-evident audit log and encrypted backups.
- Each clinic gets its own separate server and database.
- See the Security and HIPAA Overview for details, including what is not yet in place.

### What it does not do (yet)
- No electronic claims submission or clearinghouse connection. Print superbills and send charge details to your biller.
- No e-prescribing.
- No patient portal beyond phone check-in.

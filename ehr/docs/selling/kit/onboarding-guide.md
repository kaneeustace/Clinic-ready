# ChartReady Onboarding Guide: Your First Week

For the administrator at a new ChartReady clinic. Work through this list in order. Most items take a few minutes. If anything looks different from what's described here, contact [Your Company LLC] support at [support email / phone].

**Before you start**
- [Your Company LLC] sends you your clinic's ChartReady link and your first administrator login. Change that password the first time you sign in (**My account**).
- Only enter real client information after your BAA with [Your Company LLC] is signed and we've confirmed your system is ready for it.
- Use a computer for setup. Staff can use phones and tablets afterward.

---

## Day 1: Clinic setup

### 1. Clinic settings
**Where:** Administration → Clinic settings

- [ ] Clinic name
- [ ] Logo (upload)
- [ ] Address and phone
- [ ] Group NPI
- [ ] Tax ID
- [ ] Brand color

Why: these appear across the app, on printouts, superbills and emails. Superbills show a "not set" warning until the address, NPI and tax ID are filled in.

### 2. Turn on two-factor sign-in for yourself
**Where:** My account → Two-factor sign-in → Set up two-factor sign-in

- [ ] Install an authenticator app on your phone (for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password).
- [ ] Scan the code and enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
- [ ] Save your recovery codes somewhere safe and offline. Each code works once.

Two-factor sign-in is normally required for everyone at your clinic; staff are asked to set it up at their first sign-in. If you lose your phone, another administrator can reset it under Administration → Users, or contact us.

### 3. Appointment colors
**Where:** Administration → Appointment colors

- [ ] Pick a color for each appointment type (intake, therapy, psychiatry evaluation, medication management, family, group, consult). Used on the schedule, dashboard and appointment pages.

---

## Day 2: Staff and supervision

### 4. Add staff and assign roles
**Where:** Administration → Users → Add a user

For each person, enter name, email, role and (for clinicians) credentials and NPI.

| Role | Give to | Can |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | Owner, office manager | Everything, including users, audit log, security, backups, settings |
| Clinician | Therapists, counselors, prescribers, BHTs | Charts, notes, treatment plans, measures, scheduling; view billing |
| Front desk | Reception | Demographics, scheduling, check-ins, intake requests; view billing |
| Billing | Biller | Demographics, charges, payments, superbills |

- [ ] One account per person. Never share logins.
- [ ] Enter credentials exactly (for example LPC, LCSW, LAC, LMSW, BHT). ChartReady uses them to decide who needs a co-signature and, for AHCCCS, the education-level billing modifier.
- [ ] Give the fewest people the Admin role.

### 5. Supervisors and co-signatures
**Where:** Administration → Users → (person) → Edit

- [ ] For each associate-level clinician, BHT or BHPP, set **Clinical supervisor**.
- [ ] Check **Notes need supervisor co-signature**. ChartReady sets this automatically from credentials, but confirm it for every clinician against your roster.

How it works: clinicians sign their notes within 24 hours of the session; supervisors co-sign within 48 hours of that. Both show as countdowns on the dashboard workboard. If your clinic needs different time limits, ask us.

### 6. End-of-day note emails
**Where:** Administration → End-of-day note emails

- [ ] Send yourself a test email.
- [ ] Confirm the send time (default 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday). We set the email service and schedule for you; ask us to change the time or days.

Each clinician gets counts of notes to write, drafts to sign and co-signatures owed, plus a sign-in link. No patient information is included. Staff can turn it off on their own Account page.

### 7. Staff install the app
- [ ] iPhone: open your ChartReady link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
- [ ] Android: open it in Chrome → Install app.

No patient information is stored on the phone.

## Day 3: Check-in

### 8. Check-in links
**Where:** Schedule → Check-ins

- [ ] Confirm the "How clients get their check-in" card shows automatic links as on (sent by email or text about 60 minutes before each appointment, using each client's preferred contact method).
- [ ] Make sure clients' phone, email and **preferred contact** are filled in on their chart.
- [ ] Send yourself a test link from a test appointment's page.

Texting requires setup on our side (and a signed BAA with the texting provider). Ask us if links are only going out by email.

### 9. Set up a kiosk
**Where:** on the front-desk iPad, sign in, then Schedule → Check-ins → Set up a kiosk → Make this device a kiosk

- [ ] The iPad then shows the client check-in screen and resets itself between clients.
- [ ] To retire a kiosk, revoke it from the same page.

### 10. Review check-ins daily
- [ ] Front desk reviews contact-detail changes and new insurance cards on the Check-ins page. Changes aren't applied to the chart until staff approve them.
- [ ] Decide who responds when a client's screener triggers a self-harm alert, and write it down.

---

## Day 4: Clients and treatment plans

### 11. Add clients
**Where:** Patients → New patient

- [ ] Demographics, contact details and preferred contact method.
- [ ] Insurance. For Arizona Medicaid members, enter the **AHCCCS ID** (a letter followed by 8 digits). This switches on the AHCCCS documentation and billing checks for that client.
- [ ] Profile photo on the **Pictures** tab, plus photo ID and insurance cards if you collect them.
- [ ] Problem list (ICD-10 diagnoses), allergies, medications.
- [ ] Clients in a program: set their **Level of care**, and add payer authorizations with the next review date.

ChartReady warns about possible duplicates. Check before creating a second record.

### 12. Treatment plans before progress notes
**Where:** the client's chart → Treatment plan

- [ ] Write and sign a treatment plan for every active client **before** their next progress or group session. ChartReady won't let anyone start or sign a progress or group note without a signed, active plan.
- [ ] Get the client's signature, and a parent or guardian's for minors (required to activate an AHCCCS minor's plan).
- [ ] Set the review date.

Intake, crisis safety plan and ASAM notes are never blocked, so new clients can always be assessed first.

### 13. Fee schedule
**Where:** Billing → Fee schedule (administrators)

- [ ] Enter your fees for the codes you bill. The starting amounts are placeholders.

---

## Day 5: Security and backups

### 14. Backups
**Where:** Backups (left menu)

- [ ] Confirm the latest backup has a recent time and a "verified" status.
- [ ] Note who at your clinic to call if you ever need data restored: [Your Company LLC] support.

### 15. Security and audit routine
**Where:** Security (left menu); Administration → Audit log

- [ ] Look at the Security page. Acknowledge and resolve any alerts with a short note.
- [ ] Pick a regular time (for example the first Monday of each month) to review the audit log and alerts.
- [ ] Confirm every staff member has two-factor sign-in on (Administration → Users shows each person's status).

### 16. Offboarding
- [ ] When someone leaves, deactivate them the same day under Administration → Users. This signs them out everywhere.

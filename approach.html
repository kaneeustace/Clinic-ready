About us

Care that is thoughtful, coordinated and built around you

At Clinic Ready Behavioral Health, we pair proven treatments with real human connection. Here's how we think about care, and what you can expect from us.

Our mission

Helping people feel better, and stay better

Our mission is to make high-quality, evidence-based mental health care easier to find, easier to start and easier to stick with, for every person who walks through our door or logs on from home.

What that means day to day

  • We listen first and treat you as the expert on your own life
  • We use approaches backed by research, explained in plain language
  • We keep your care team talking to each other
  • We check in on how things are going and adjust when something isn't working
Core values

What guides our work

These values shape every visit, every care plan and every conversation with our team.

Evidence-based

We use treatments that research shows can help, such as CBT, DBT, EMDR and established medication options, and we tailor them to your goals rather than following a script.

Trauma-informed

We recognize that hard experiences shape how people feel and respond. You set the pace, you have choices at every step, and you never have to share more than you're ready to.

Collaborative

Care is something we do with you, not to you. We set goals together, share what we're noticing and make decisions as a team, with family or other supports involved if you'd like.

Culturally responsive & LGBTQ+ affirming

Your identity, culture, faith, language and lived experience matter. We respect your name and pronouns, and we work to understand what wellbeing looks like for you.

Accessible

Good care should be within reach. We offer telehealth, evening and Saturday hours, help with insurance questions and accommodations for disabilities, no explanation needed.

Questions about fit?

If you're not sure we're the right place for you, ask. A care coordinator is happy to talk it through, with no pressure.

Measurement-based care

We check in on progress, together

Feeling better can be hard to notice from the inside. So every few sessions, we'll ask you to fill out a short questionnaire, usually just a few minutes long, about things like mood, worry, sleep and day-to-day functioning.

Your clinician reviews the results with you. If things are improving, great: we keep going. If they're not, that's useful information too, and we'll talk about adjusting the plan.

What to know about check-ins

  • They're brief and use widely used, standardized questionnaires
  • You can complete them online before a visit or in the office
  • There are no right or wrong answers, and they are never a test
  • Your answers are part of your confidential health record
  • You can always ask to see your results or skip a question
Integrated care

Therapy and psychiatry, working as one team

When you see both a therapist and a psychiatric provider with us, they coordinate, so you don't have to repeat your story or carry messages between them.

Shared plan

With your permission, your providers share notes and agree on goals, so therapy and medication support each other.

Regular coordination

Your team checks in with each other when something changes, such as a new medication, a tough stretch or a big step forward.

Your outside providers

If you'd like, we can also coordinate with your primary care doctor, school or other providers, only with your written consent.

Your care over time

What a care plan looks like

Every plan is personal, but most follow a similar rhythm. Here's a general picture.

  1. Getting to know you

    In your first visits, we learn about what brought you in, your history, strengths and what you'd like to be different.

  2. Setting goals

    Together we agree on a few clear, meaningful goals and choose the approaches most likely to help.

  3. Doing the work

    Regular sessions focused on skills, insight and support, with brief check-ins to see how things are moving.

  4. Reviewing & adjusting

    Every so often we step back and look at progress. We may change frequency, try a new approach or add services.

  5. Planning for what's next

    As goals are met, we taper visits, plan for setbacks and make sure you know you can come back any time.

An honest note about scope

What we are, and what we're not

We are an outpatient clinic

We provide scheduled therapy, psychiatric care and group programs, in person and by telehealth. This works well for many people managing a wide range of concerns while living their everyday lives.

We are not an emergency or inpatient service

We don't offer 24/7 crisis response, hospital care, residential treatment or medical detox. If you need more support than outpatient care can safely provide, we'll help you find and connect with the right level of care, and stay in touch where we can.

Need help right now? Call or text 988 any time, or call 911 in an emergency. See our crisis resources for more options.

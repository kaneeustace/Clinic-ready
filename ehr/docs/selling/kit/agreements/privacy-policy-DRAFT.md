[Your Company LLC] ("**we**," "**us**") provides ChartReady, a hosted electronic health record service for behavioral health clinics. This policy explains what information we collect through our website [website URL] and in running our business, how we use it, and how it differs from the patient information our clinic customers store in ChartReady.

---

## 1. Two kinds of information

**Vendor information (covered by this policy).** Information about visitors to our website, prospective customers, and the staff of our customer clinics in their role as account users and business contacts. Examples: your name, work email, phone, clinic name, and messages you send us.

**Customer patient information (not covered by this policy).** Clinics enter information about their clients into ChartReady, including protected health information (PHI). That information belongs to the clinic. We handle it only as the clinic's business associate under a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** and HIPAA, and only to provide the service. If you are a patient, the clinic's Notice of Privacy Practices explains how your information is used; please contact your clinic with questions or requests about your records.

- Billing details for your subscription. [If a payment processor is used: card or bank details are collected and stored by [processor], not by us.]
- The content of your support requests. *We ask customers not to send patient information by email or support request; if any is included, it is treated under the BAA.*

**Collected automatically on our website:**
- Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, in server logs [and through [analytics tool], if used].
- [Cookies: list any. *Counsel/owner: avoid third-party tracking pixels and ad trackers on any page with forms, consistent with HHS guidance on online tracking technologies.*]

**From customer staff using ChartReady (account data):**
- User account information (name, email, role, credentials, NPI) and sign-in and security records (for example audit log entries, failed sign-ins). This data is stored within the customer's ChartReady instance and is handled under the customer agreement and BAA.
- Non-identifying operational metrics about each customer's instance (for example counts of users and signed notes, last sign-in time, software version, backup status) that contain no patient information, used to monitor service health.

## 3. How we use vendor information

- To respond to inquiries, schedule demos and provide support.
- To set up and manage accounts, invoice and collect payment.
- To operate, secure and improve our website and service.
- To send service notices (for example maintenance or security notices) and, with your permission or where allowed by law, occasional product updates. You can opt out of marketing email at any time.
- To comply with law and enforce our agreements.

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use customer patient information for marketing, advertising or product promotion.

## 4. How we share vendor information

- **Service providers** who help us run the business (for example hosting, email, payment processing, accounting), under contracts that limit their use of the information.
- **Professional advisers** such as attorneys, accountants and insurers.
- **Legal requirements:** when required by law, subpoena or to protect rights and safety.
- **Business transfers:** in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy. Customer patient information would transfer only together with the obligations of the applicable BAA.

## 5. Email and text messages sent by ChartReady

Emails ChartReady sends on a clinic's behalf (such as end-of-day reminders to staff and check-in links to clients) are designed to contain no patient health information: counts and a sign-in link, or the clinic name and a check-in link. Text messages, when a clinic enables them, contain the clinic name and a link only.

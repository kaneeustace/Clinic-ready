## 1. Definitions

**(a) Catch-all definition.** The following terms used in this Agreement have the same meaning as in the HIPAA Rules: Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Protected Health Information, Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use.

**(b) Specific definitions.**
- **Business Associate** has the meaning given in 45 CFR 160.103 and, in reference to this Agreement, means [Your Company LLC].
- **Covered Entity** has the meaning given in 45 CFR 160.103 and, in reference to this Agreement, means [Customer legal name].
- **HIPAA Rules** means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification and Enforcement Rules at 45 CFR Part 160 and Part 164.
- **Protected Health Information** or **PHI** means Protected Health Information, including electronic PHI, that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity.

- **Unsuccessful Security Incidents.** The parties agree that this section serves as notice of the ongoing existence of unsuccessful Security Incidents (such as pings, port scans, blocked login attempts and denial-of-service attempts that do not result in unauthorized access, use or disclosure), for which no further notice is required.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures by Business Associate

**(a) Services.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as necessary to perform the services set out in the Underlying Agreement: hosting, operating, maintaining, securing, backing up, supporting and restoring the ChartReady service for Covered Entity [and, if enabled by Covered Entity, sending note content to an AI Subcontractor for documentation review and sending check-in messages through a messaging Subcontractor].

**(b) De-identification.** Business Associate [may / may not] de-identify PHI in accordance with 45 CFR 164.514(a)–(c). *[Counsel: recommend "may not" unless there is a defined need.]*

**(c) Required by Law.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required by Law.

**(d) Minimum necessary.** Business Associate agrees to make uses, disclosures and requests for PHI consistent with Covered Entity's minimum necessary policies and procedures.

**(e) No impermissible uses.** Business Associate may not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except for the specific uses and disclosures in (f) through (h) below.

**(f) Management and administration.** Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities.

**(g) Disclosures for management.** Business Associate may disclose PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, provided the disclosures are Required by Law, or Business Associate obtains reasonable assurances from the person to whom the information is disclosed that it will remain confidential and be used or further disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purposes for which it was disclosed, and the person notifies Business Associate of any instances of which it is aware in which the confidentiality of the information has been breached.

**(h) Data aggregation.** Business Associate [may / may not] provide Data Aggregation services relating to the Health Care Operations of Covered Entity. *[Counsel: recommend "may not"; the Service is single-tenant per clinic.]*

**(i) No sale or marketing.** Business Associate will not sell PHI or use or disclose PHI for marketing.

## 4. Provisions for Covered Entity to inform Business Associate of privacy practices and restrictions

**(a)** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation(s) in its Notice of Privacy Practices under 45 CFR 164.520, to the extent that such limitation may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

**(b)** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any changes in, or revocation of, the permission by an Individual to use or disclose his or her PHI, to the extent that such changes may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

**(c)** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by under 45 CFR 164.522, to the extent that such restriction may affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

## 5. Permissible requests by Covered Entity

Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by Covered Entity [except as permitted in Section 3(f)–(h)].

Covered Entity is responsible for: its own HIPAA compliance; managing its users' accounts and roles in the Service; obtaining any consents or authorizations required for its disclosures; and not enabling optional features (such as AI review or text messaging) for PHI until Business Associate confirms the relevant Subcontractor agreement is in place.

**(a) Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and terminates on the date Covered Entity terminates it for cause under (b), or [when the Underlying Agreement ends and all PHI has been returned or destroyed under (c)], whichever is sooner.

**(b) Termination for cause.** Business Associate authorizes termination of this Agreement by Covered Entity if Covered Entity determines Business Associate has violated a material term of this Agreement [and Business Associate has not cured the breach or ended the violation within [30] days after written notice]. *[Counsel: choose between immediate termination and a cure period.]*

**(c) Obligations of Business Associate upon termination.** Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Business Associate, with respect to PHI received from Covered Entity, or created, maintained or received by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity, shall:
1. Retain only that PHI which is necessary for Business Associate to continue its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities;
2. Return to Covered Entity [or, if agreed to by Covered Entity, destroy] the remaining PHI that Business Associate still maintains in any form, in [format] within [N] days, following the transition and export process in the Underlying Agreement;
3. Continue to use appropriate safeguards and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 with respect to electronic PHI to prevent use or disclosure of the PHI, other than as provided for in this Section, for as long as Business Associate retains the PHI;
4. Not use or disclose the PHI retained by Business Associate other than for the purposes for which such PHI was retained and subject to the same conditions set out in Section 3(f) and (g) which applied prior to termination;
5. Return to Covered Entity [or destroy] the PHI retained by Business Associate when it is no longer needed for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities; and
6. Ensure that encrypted backups containing PHI are deleted or allowed to expire under the backup retention schedule within [N] days, and certify destruction in writing on request.

## 8. 42 CFR Part 2 (substance use disorder records): questions for counsel

Many Arizona outpatient and IOP clinics provide substance use disorder (SUD) treatment. If Covered Entity is a federally assisted "Part 2 program," its SUD records are also governed by 42 CFR Part 2, which was substantially revised by the 2024 final rule aligning parts of Part 2 with HIPAA.

*Counsel should advise on:*
- Whether Covered Entity is a Part 2 program, and whether Business Associate is a "qualified service organization" (QSO) that also needs a **qualified service organization agreement (QSOA)**, or whether QSOA terms should be added to this Agreement.
- Business Associate's acknowledgment that, in receiving, storing, processing or otherwise dealing with Part 2 records, it is fully bound by Part 2, and will resist in judicial proceedings any efforts to obtain access to Part 2 records except as permitted by Part 2.
- Redisclosure restrictions, the required notice accompanying disclosures (the Service displays a Part 2 redisclosure notice on flagged releases), and breach notification for Part 2 records under the current rule.
- Compliance dates and any transition provisions under the current version of Part 2.
- Arizona-specific confidentiality rules for behavioral health records and minors.

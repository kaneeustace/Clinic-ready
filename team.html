Finding the right fit

How we match you with a clinician

A good fit matters. Research shows the relationship with your clinician is one of the strongest predictors of progress.

You're always welcome to request a different clinician, at any time and for any reason. Just let us know. No explanation needed, and no hard feelings.

  1. Tell us what you need

    During your free 15-minute phone consult, we'll ask what brings you in and what matters to you in a clinician.

  2. We suggest a match

    We consider specialty, approach, availability, insurance and any preferences, like gender, language or telehealth.

  3. Check in after a few sessions

    If it doesn't feel right, we'll help you switch. Your care continues without starting over.

How we help

Care for the whole person

Whether you're working through a hard season or managing a long-term condition, we'll help you find the right kind of support.

Individual therapy

One-on-one sessions using CBT, DBT, EMDR and other proven approaches for anxiety, depression, trauma and life transitions.

Psychiatry & medication

Getting started

Starting care is simple

Three steps from first contact to your first session.

  1. Reach out

    Request an appointment online or call us. A care coordinator will get back to you within one business day.

  2. Brief phone consult

    A free 15-minute call to understand what you're looking for, check insurance and match you with the right clinician.

  3. First appointment

    Meet your clinician in person or by video. Together you'll set goals and build a plan that fits you.

# ChartReady Sales Kit

Documents for selling, demoing and launching ChartReady with small outpatient and IOP clinics, starting in Arizona. Feature claims are based on what the software does today; check `features.md` before promising anything new.

## For prospects

- [one-pager.md](one-pager.md): one-page summary of who it's for, the problem, what ChartReady does, launch pricing and the next step.
- [features.md](features.md): fuller feature list by area, plus what is not available today.
- [security-overview.md](security-overview.md): security and HIPAA answers for a clinic's compliance person, including what is not yet in place.

## For selling

- [demo-script.md](demo-script.md): 20-minute live demo using the public demo, with talking points and honest answers to common objections.

## For new customers

- [onboarding-guide.md](onboarding-guide.md): first-week checklist for a new clinic's administrator, with where each setting lives in the app.

## For the owner

- [owner-launch-checklist.md](owner-launch-checklist.md): business, legal, vendor and security steps to finish before taking money.

## Agreements (drafts for attorney review; do not sign or publish as-is)

- [agreements/terms-of-service-DRAFT.md](agreements/terms-of-service-DRAFT.md): subscription terms: fees, data ownership, export, acceptable use, availability, support, warranties, liability.
- [agreements/business-associate-agreement-DRAFT.md](agreements/business-associate-agreement-DRAFT.md): BAA following the HHS sample provisions (45 CFR 164.504(e)), with 42 CFR Part 2 questions for counsel.
- [agreements/order-form-DRAFT.md](agreements/order-form-DRAFT.md): customer details, plan, clinicians, term, fees, start date, design-partner terms and signatures.
- [agreements/privacy-policy-DRAFT.md](agreements/privacy-policy-DRAFT.md): website and vendor privacy policy, separating vendor data from customer PHI governed by the BAA.
